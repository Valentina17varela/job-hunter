## Brand & Style

The design system drives a crisp, high-velocity job hunting and career progression platform. Built to counter the fatigue of modern career searches, the visual narrative centers on clarity, optimism, and executive efficiency. The UI balances an ultra-clean corporate productivity surface with warm, radiant energy—moving away from sterile applicant tracking tools and dense terminal aesthetics toward an approachable, forward-looking product experience.

The aesthetic fuses modern minimalism with subtle tactile warmth:
- **Clean Architectural White:** Extensive, intentional breathing room using pure white canvas foundations and subtle light-slate surfaces.
- **Warm Kinetic Orange Accents:** Punchy, high-energy orange tones inject optimism into key calls to action, active application states, interview flags, and match highlights.
- **Deep Slate Typography:** High-contrast, near-black characters ensure sustained legibility across dense resumes, job descriptions, and salary benchmarks.
- **Refined Rounded Structures:** Generous, soft contours and subtle outline borders evoke precision without clinical coldness.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for a high-contrast light mode experience, establishing an energetic hierarchy across application queues, match algorithms, and communication panels.

- **Primary (`#f97316` / Kinetic Orange):** The signature driver of action. Governs primary conversion funnels, applied status pills, urgent alerts, and focal call-to-actions. Supported by `#ea580c` for interactive pressed/hover states and `#fff7ed` for tinted badge fills.
- **Secondary (`#0f172a` / Deep Slate):** Anchors layout structure, high-emphasis interactive badges, dark contextual tooltips, and secondary navigation anchors.
- **Tertiary (`#0284c7` / Sky Blue):** Dedicated to auxiliary informational metrics, salary ranges, external company links, and recruiter profile tags.
- **Neutral Surfaces (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`):** Provide layered depth without dark shadows. Pure `#ffffff` defines primary actionable cards, while `#f8fafc` and `#f1f5f9` define workspace backgrounds and inactive panels.
- **Borders & Outlines (`#e2e8f0`):** Precise 1px borders anchor elements to the grid cleanly without heavy drop shadows.

## Typography

The typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans across all roles to project an approachable, tech-forward, and human personality. Its rounded geometric construction softens dense tabular information, keeping large volumes of job descriptions, stage pipelines, and resume bullets pleasant to read.

- **Headlines & Metric Callouts:** Rendered with semi-bold to extra-bold weights (`600`–`800`) with tight letter spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`), providing punchy structure to job titles, match scores, and section anchors.
- **Body & Notes:** Configured with balanced line heights (`1.5`–`1.65`) for effortless scanning during prolonged research and cover letter reviews.
- **Labels & Micro-UI:** Set with elevated weights (`600`) and slight letter-spacing expansions to maintain legibility inside status badges, chips, filter tabs, and form indicators.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be used for salaries, application days active, and percentage metrics to ensure layout alignment across lists.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean tonal layering and low-contrast outlines rather than muddy, dark shadows, keeping the entire interface luminous and modern:

- **Canvas Foundation:** Base viewport sits on `#f8fafc` or pure `#ffffff`.
- **Card Containers (Level 1):** Solid white (`#ffffff`) background bounded by a sharp, crisp 1px outline in `#e2e8f0`. Resting cards use zero shadow or an ultra-diffused tint: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive Hover & Focal States (Level 2):** On hover or focus, cards elevate slightly via an ambient micro-shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with the border shifting from `#e2e8f0` to `#fdba74`.
- **Floating Overlays & Popovers (Level 3):** Dropdown menus, modal dialogs, and sticky action docks float with `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` backed by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and an outline in `#cbd5e1`.
- **Emissive Highlights:** Reserved exclusively for active orange CTA buttons and high-affinity match percentages using a warm halo: `0 4px 14px 0 rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced rounded geometry to create a friendly, approachable, yet structured product personality:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to standard interactive controls, input fields, checkboxes, and individual list items.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** Standard for job cards, filter panels, application preview modules, and metrics blocks.
- **Surface Modal Radius (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Used on global search command bars, modal overlays, application flow dialogs, and hero banners.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Strictly reserved for chips, metadata badges (salary, remote, full-time), avatar containers, and floating filter trigger tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid warm orange (`#f97316`) fill with crisp white text (`#ffffff`), `font-weight: 600`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover shifts to `#ea580c` with `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px 0 rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.35)`. Active state scales subtly (`scale: 0.98`).
- **Secondary Action:** Crisp white (`#ffffff`) background, 1px border `#e2e8f0`, text `#111827`. Hover elevates background to `#f8fafc` and border to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#475569`. Hover provides a soft wash of `#fff7ed` with text transitioning to `#ea580c`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Format:** Full rounded edges (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, `label-md` typography.
- **Match Score Badge:** Soft orange fill (`#fff7ed`), border `1px solid #fed7aa`, energetic orange text (`#ea580c`).
- **Metadata Tag (Remote, Full-time):** Light slate background (`#f1f5f9`), neutral border (`#e2e8f0`), slate text (`#475569`).
- **Interactive Filter Chip:** White fill, subtle border, turns to solid `#0f172a` with white text when active, accompanied by an instant dismiss icon.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Crisp `#ffffff` container, 1px border `#e2e8f0`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, typography `body-md` in `#111827`. Placeholder in `#94a3b8`.
- Focus ring: Border switches cleanly to `#f97316` accompanied by an outer warm glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.15)`.
- Global search hero field includes integrated left search icon and an embedded right-aligned orange CTA button (`Apply Filters`).

### Job Listing Cards
- Clean `#ffffff` background with 1px `#e2e8f0` outline and `1rem` corner radius.
- Padding structured at `1.25rem`.
- Features clear metadata hierarchy: Company logo & verified mark top left, Match Affinity Pill top right, bold job title in `#111827`, salary and location line in `#475569`, followed by a horizontal row of pill badges and a quick-save heart toggle.
- Hover state softly shifts border to `#fdba74` and lifts card with `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.08)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Toggles
- Custom `18px` rounded controls (`rounded: 4px` for checkboxes, `rounded-full` for radios).
- Unchecked: Border `1.5px solid #cbd5e1`, white background.
- Checked: Solid `#f97316` fill, white checkmark glyph or center dot, zero harsh outlines.

### Lists & Activity Timeline
- Inset within `#f8fafc` container wells or isolated with hairline dividers (`1px solid #f1f5f9`).
- Application milestone dots utilize energetic orange nodes with connecting lines rendered in soft slate `#e2e8f0`.
